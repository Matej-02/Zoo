Welcome to Pocket Zoo Deluxe

Create enclosed habitats, care for animals, attract visitors and grow from a small roadside zoo into a five-star park.

1. Build access
Extend the entrance path so guests can reach exhibits and facilities.
2. Close the habitat
Use fences and at least one gate. Paths alone do not count as animal barriers.
3. Meet animal needs
Each species needs enough space, companions, water, shelter or foliage.
4. Manage the zoo
Hire staff, add food and toilets, complete goals and unlock new species.

Tip: Start with a small zebra habitat next to the entrance path. Add two zebras, one water tile, one tree, a shelter and a feeder.
